# Hydration Reference Guide

Water is the main chemical component of the body, making up about 60% of body weight. Proper hydration is critical for regulating temperature, flushing waste, and lubricating joints.

## Recommended Fluid Intake
Daily fluid requirements depend on activity level, climate, and overall health. Standard guidelines are:
- **Men**: Approximately 3.7 liters (125 ounces) of total fluids per day, including beverages and food water content.
- **Women**: Approximately 2.7 liters (91 ounces) of total fluids per day.
- Direct pure water target is typically around 2.0 to 3.0 liters daily for active adults.

## Signs of Dehydration
Mild to moderate dehydration presents as dry mouth, fatigue, headache, and dark-colored urine. Severe dehydration requires immediate medical attention. Monitoring urine color (aiming for pale yellow) is a reliable indicator of proper hydration status.
