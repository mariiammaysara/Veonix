# Portion Control Reference Guide

Managing portion sizes is key to healthy eating and weight management. Using visual cues and structured plating methods helps prevent unintentional overeating.

## The Plate Method
The plate method is an easy visual guide to structure balanced meals without tracking exact weights:
- **Half the plate (50%)**: Fill with non-starchy vegetables (e.g., spinach, broccoli, peppers, salad) to provide fiber, vitamins, and minerals with low calories.
- **One-quarter (25%)**: Fill with lean protein (e.g., chicken, fish, tofu, beans) to maintain muscle mass and satiety.
- **One-quarter (25%)**: Fill with complex carbohydrates or whole grains (e.g., brown rice, quinoa, whole-wheat pasta, sweet potato) for sustained energy.

## Hand Guidelines for Portions
When eating out, hands can serve as approximate measuring tools:
- **Palm size**: Approximate size for a portion of protein (approx 3 ounces or 85g).
- **Fist size**: Approximate size for a portion of vegetables or fruit (1 cup).
- **Cupped hand**: Approximate size for a portion of complex carbs (1/2 cup).
- **Thumb size**: Approximate size for high-fat foods or oils (1 tablespoon).
