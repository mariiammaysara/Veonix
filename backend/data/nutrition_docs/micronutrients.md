# Micronutrients Reference Guide

Micronutrients, consisting of vitamins and minerals, are required by the body in small amounts but are essential for physiological processes, disease prevention, and overall well-being.

## Vitamins
Vitamins are organic compounds categorized into fat-soluble and water-soluble:
- **Fat-soluble**: Vitamins A, D, E, and K are stored in liver and fatty tissues.
- **Water-soluble**: Vitamin C and B-complex vitamins dissolve in water and are not stored, requiring daily intake.

## Minerals
Minerals are inorganic elements essential for bone structure, enzyme cofactors, and electrolyte balance:
- **Calcium**: Essential for bone density and muscle contraction.
- **Iron**: Critical for oxygen transport via hemoglobin.
- **Sodium**: Regulates blood volume and pressure, but intake should be limited. The maximum recommended daily sodium intake for adults is 2,300 milligrams (mg) to prevent cardiovascular issues.
- **Potassium**: Counteracts sodium's effect on blood pressure and assists nerve signals.
