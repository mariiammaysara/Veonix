# Macronutrients Reference Guide

Macronutrients are nutrients that the body needs in large amounts to provide energy and maintain structure and function. They consist of carbohydrates, proteins, and fats.

## Carbohydrates
Carbohydrates are the body's primary energy source. They are broken down into glucose, which fuels cell and brain activity. One gram of carbohydrates yields 4 kilocalories (kcal) of energy.
Daily recommendation is typically 45% to 65% of total daily energy intake. Healthy choices include whole grains, vegetables, and fruits.

## Proteins
Proteins are essential for building, repairing, and maintaining tissues such as muscle, skin, and organs. They are composed of amino acids. One gram of protein yields 4 kcal of energy.
Daily recommended intake is typically 10% to 35% of total calories, or about 0.8 grams per kilogram of body weight for a sedentary adult. Lean sources include chicken, turkey, fish, beans, and eggs.

## Fats
Fats are vital for hormone production, cell membrane integrity, and absorbing fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). One gram of fat yields 9 kcal of energy.
Daily recommendation is 20% to 35% of daily energy intake, focusing on monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats like olive oil, avocados, nuts, and seeds. Saturated fat should be limited.
