# Calorie Deficit Reference Guide

Weight management is primarily governed by the principle of energy balance: calories consumed versus calories expended. A calorie deficit is required for fat loss.

## Achieving a Calorie Deficit
A calorie deficit occurs when energy expenditure exceeds energy intake. It can be achieved through:
1. **Dietary adjustments**: Reducing portion sizes, choosing nutrient-dense but low-calorie foods.
2. **Physical activity**: Increasing daily movement, strength training, and cardiovascular exercises.
3. **Combination**: The most sustainable approach combines small calorie reductions with increased daily activity.

## Healthy Deficit Guidelines
A safe and sustainable calorie deficit for weight loss is 300 to 500 calories per day below maintenance needs. This typically leads to a fat loss of 0.5 to 1.0 pound (approx 0.25 to 0.5 kg) per week. Extreme deficits below 1,200 calories per day for women or 1,500 calories per day for men can result in muscle loss, nutrient deficiencies, and metabolic slowdown.
